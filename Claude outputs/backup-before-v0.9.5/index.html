Unicorn Adventure

A cotton-candy world to explore
Space jumps, hold it in the air to glide. Shift gallops. Click and drag to look around.

How to play

↑ ↓ ← →Ride the way you push: Left goes left, Down turns around, and the view swings round to follow (W A S D work too) ShiftGallop! Sparkles and a rainbow trail SpaceJump. Press again in the air to flap, hold to glide EPet a nearby friend, or say hi to a townsperson (the outlined card) TabSwitch between friends when several are close. You can also click or tap a card FFeed a treat. Friends love them, and you never run out! RPlay together with a friend QCast your horn magic CChange spell (or press 1 to 4). New spells unlock as you make friends NSkip to sunrise, day, sunset or starry night BOpen your friends book PDream ride: sit back while your unicorn explores by itself VTry the map view (looking down from above, arrow keys go North, South, East and West). Press V again to go back GGraphics: Fancy (inked cartoon outlines) or Simple (a little faster on slow devices) MouseDrag to look around, scroll to zoom

Gamepad works too: A jumps, X is magic, B pets, Y feeds, bumper plays, d-pad right changes spell, d-pad left switches who you talk to.

Make friends with every creature, collect gumdrops from the colorful bushes for wishes, and fly through the rainbow rings!

Little wonders to find: bounce on the big mushroom caps, ride into glowing fairy rings, toss two gumdrops into the wishing fountain (press E), and ride across the rainbow chimes. Wish on shooting stars at night!

Twinkle Town is a little way west of where you start: a cobbled Main Street, a plaza and fountain, and shopkeepers who live there. Say hi to the baker for a treat, and ask Pixel the fox about the mail. Everyone goes to bed when the stars come out!
