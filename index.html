Welcome back, Sparkle!

Space jumps, hold it in the air to glide. Shift gallops. Click and drag to look around.

Make your unicorn!

How to play

↑ ↓ ← →Ride the way you push: Left goes left, Down turns around, and the view swings round to follow (W A S D work too) ShiftGallop! Sparkles and a rainbow trail SpaceJump. Press again in the air to flap, hold to glide EPet a nearby friend, or say hi to a townsperson (the big card). You can also just tap the card TabSwitch who you talk to when several friends are close (the others become little name tags you can tap) FFeed a treat. Friends love them, and you never run out! RPlay together once you are friends QCast your horn magic CChange spell (or press 1 to 4). New spells unlock as you make friends NSkip to sunrise, day, sunset or starry night LDress up your unicorn: coat, mane, horn, sparkles, cutie mark, saddle and size (or tap the brush button at the top right). Look for gift boxes out in the world: each one has something new to wear! JAsk the princess on your saddle to hop off (or tap "Hop off" at the top left). When she is waiting for you, press E on her card to ask her to ride with you BOpen your friends book PDream ride: sit back while your unicorn explores by itself VTry the map view (looking down from above, arrow keys go North, South, East and West). Press V again to go back GGraphics: Fancy (inked cartoon outlines) or Simple (a little faster on slow devices) MouseDrag to look around, scroll to zoom

Gamepad works too: A jumps, X is magic, B pets, Y feeds, bumper plays, d-pad right changes spell, d-pad left switches who you talk to.

Make friends with every creature, collect gumdrops from the colorful bushes, and fly through the rainbow rings!

Little wonders to find: bounce on the big mushroom caps, ride into glowing fairy rings, make a free wish at the wishing fountain (press E), and ride across the rainbow chimes. Wish on shooting stars at night!

Twinkle Town is a little way west of where you start: a cobbled Main Street, a plaza and fountain, and shopkeepers who live there. Say hi to the baker for a treat, and ask Pixel the fox about the mail. Everyone goes to bed when the stars come out!
